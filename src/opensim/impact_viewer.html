Elastic plate drop test

__LIMITS__

Four simply supported edges; centered square contact patch. Time starts at first touch. The drop height sets the initial downward speed. Gravity acts on the drop weight; plate self-weight is omitted. Contact stiffness represents a compliant interface; it is not a measured material property.

Force, displacement and energy histories

__ACCURACY__

Results and files

Use Export drop-test history in the desktop for CSV. The job folder also contains history.csv, impact.npz and manifest.json. Reported peaks are tracked at every integration step; plots and animation are sampled. No stress, failure or damage result is calculated.
